## 1. Purpose

This charter establishes the **AML/CFT Oversight Committee** of **Xego**, with responsibility for overseeing the adequacy and effectiveness of the AML/CFT program and reporting to the Board of Directors.

## 2. Mandate

The Committee shall:

1. Oversee implementation of the AML/CFT Compliance Policy and Program.
2. Review the institutional ML/FT risk assessment and its timely refresh.
3. Review STR/CTR filing activity, PEP relationships and sanctions screening outcomes.
4. Monitor the KYC/CDD/EDD regime, including the L0–L4 ladder and identity verification provider performance.
5. Track regulatory obligations, examinations, and changes to CBN directives, FATF recommendations and MLPA.
6. Oversee independent testing of the AML/CFT program and remediation of findings.
7. Escalate material or unresolved matters to the Board.

## 3. Composition

- **Chair**: a non-executive director (or the Board chair where required).
- **Members**: Chief Executive Officer (or delegate), Chief Compliance Officer (secretary), Chief Financial Officer, Head of Risk, Head of Operations, Head of Technology/Security.
- Independent members/consultants may be co-opted as needed.
- The Committee has authority to obtain independent legal, audit or advisory support at Xego's expense.

## 4. Meetings

- At least **quarterly**; additional meetings as required (e.g., material STR activity, regulatory notice).
- Quorum: Chair (or alternate) plus two members.
- Decisions by simple majority; minutes are recorded and tabled at the Board.

## 5. Reporting and Escalation

- Minutes and key metrics (STRs filed, CTRs, PEP relationships, screening hits, KYC approval rates, verification provider uptime) are reported to the Board.
- **Immediate escalation** to the Board and CEO for: suspected sanctions violations, regulatory notices or examinations, systemic KYC/AML control failures, or evidence of staff complicity.

## 6. Authority and Independence

- The Committee's access to records, systems and personnel is unrestricted for its mandate.
- The CCO reports functionally to the Committee and has direct access to the Board on AML/CFT matters.

## 7. Review of Charter

This charter is reviewed annually or upon changes to regulation or governance structure.
