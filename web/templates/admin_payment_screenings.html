Payment screening

Payment-time counterparty screening on the money-out rails (spec G2). Ambiguous hits hold the movement with its allowance reserved; confirmed hits are interdicted outright.

Open holds

Review each ambiguous screen and dispose of it. Release re-queues the payout for dispatch (the screening is marked manually cleared). Reject fails it permanently, releases the reserved allowance, and reopens the batch so it can be settled to a different destination.
