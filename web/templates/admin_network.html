Identity rings

What this view shows

Identities linked by a shared fact: the same browser fingerprint, the same settlement bank account behind two merchants, a recipient bank account saved by two users, or one channel identifier value under two identifier columns. Each connected group is a ring, scored for triage: account edges weigh more than a shared browser, repeated and recent sightings weigh more than old ones, and confirmed alerts multiply the score. Rings with a confirmed alert among their members raise one linked case covering the whole member set.
