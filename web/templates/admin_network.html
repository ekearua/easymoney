Identity rings

What this view shows

Identities linked by a shared fact: the same browser fingerprint (device), the same settlement bank account behind two merchants, or one channel identifier value appearing under two identifier columns. Each connected group of two or more identities is a ring — the mule-cluster pattern. Shared facts are legitimate sometimes (a family browser, a business owner with two merchants); rings are where to look, not verdicts.
