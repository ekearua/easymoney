Onboarding reminders

What this is

The chat-side nudge that reaches users whose onboarding or merchant registration stalled. The sweep runs on its own 6-hour ticker and only ever sends one message per stage attempt; anything outside Meta's 24-hour WhatsApp service window needs an approved template, and with ONBOARDING_REMINDER_TEMPLATE unset the attempt is recorded as pending_template and skipped instead of burning the number's quality rating. This panel is read-only — a bug here cannot corrupt onboarding state.

Deliverability guard

When the recent WhatsApp failure/delivery ratio meets or exceeds ONBOARDING_REMINDER_DELIVERY_GUARD_FAILURE_RATIO over the last ONBOARDING_REMINDER_DELIVERY_GUARD_WINDOW, the reminder sweep is paused for the current tick so a degraded number is not hammered. Zero-disabled.
