Restricted funds

Programs

A program is restricted money you sponsor: you fund it, enrolled beneficiaries each hold a purse, and that purse can only be spent at the merchants on this allow-list. Money in a purse cannot be withdrawn or sent to another person — it is a separate ledger account precisely so that is true by construction.
